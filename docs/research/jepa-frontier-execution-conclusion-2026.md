# JEPA frontier execution conclusion, July 2026

## Decision

**Do not deploy any tested JEPA recipe into the real-world paging path.**

The materially distinct, currently runnable omissions identified in the
[frontier audit](jepa-frontier-technique-audit-2026.md) have all been
implemented, reviewed, run, and retained. None passed its frozen combination
of observable safety, mechanism, and operational value gates. No recipe
earned multiple-seed robustness, fresh sealed confirmation, or sustained
shadow evaluation.

The strongest current deployment candidates are not JEPAs:

1. keep rank-32 contractive raw dynamics as the predictive shadow baseline;
2. keep direct raw/PCA retrieval as the investigation baseline; and
3. use the existing explicit calibration, abstention, and policy contracts
   around those baselines before considering paging.

This is a bounded negative conclusion for the deliberately small, one-stack,
mostly deterministic deployment boundary. The single-stack scope is a product
constraint, not a corpus defect and not a reason to acquire unrelated stacks.
It is not a claim that JEPA fails generally.

## What is technically promising

Several ideas worked as mechanisms but not as alerting systems:

- **HEPA's output contract** is the best match to paging because it emits a
  monotone horizon-conditioned event probability. The tested representation
  and event policy did not earn deployment, but this is still the right
  interface if a more diverse event corpus becomes available.
- **Delta-JEPA** proved action information can be forced into displacement,
  but ordinary endpoint state retained more useful action information.
- **Whole-entity Causal-JEPA masking** learned cross-entity completion beyond
  persistence, but not beyond a matched coordinate mask.
- **SPHERE-JEPA and KerJEPA** materially altered the measured geometry,
  showing that their regularizers were active. Rank did not translate into
  state or effect value.
- **MoP-JEPA** genuinely specialized hard heads and remained tiny and fast
  enough for an edge process. The alternatives were not better conditional
  futures than a static codebook or raw dynamics.

These are research signals, not promotion candidates. Edge feasibility,
non-collapse, rank, or a lower self-supervised loss never overrides failed
observable and policy gates.

## Recurring reason for rejection

Across otherwise different objectives, the learned bottleneck repeatedly
discarded entity-local state or action effect that was directly available to
the raw model. Three patterns recur:

1. **The corpus is close to deterministic after declared controls.** Raw
   low-rank transitions and even a static future codebook score extremely
   well. Added probabilistic heads mostly model optimization variation rather
   than irreducible futures.
2. **Representation diagnostics are not operational value.** Effective rank,
   state probes, event-sensitive angles, head diversity, and completion can
   improve while transfer effects, attribution, calibration, or alert policy
   degrade.
3. **The product intentionally targets one logical stack family.** Cross-stack
   invariance is therefore out of scope. A useful candidate must exploit
   within-stack randomized interventions, topology changes, operational
   identities, or missing/recovery behavior without making additional stacks
   a prerequisite.

The experiments therefore falsify “try another generic JEPA objective on the
same targets” as the next best action. They do not close objectives that use
the corpus's matched interventions or the bounded alert/effect decision as
the target equivalence relation. The
[2026-07-29 exhaustion refresh](jepa-frontier-exhaustion-refresh-2026-07-29.md)
records those remaining mechanisms and their controls.

## Frontier edge-runnable techniques actually tried

The retained implementations cover:

- EMA and non-EMA masked latent prediction;
- predictor-free multi-view LeJEPA and exact Epps-Pulley SIGReg;
- ambient, subspace, rectified, kernel, hyperspherical, and deterministic
  spherical distribution matching;
- horizon-conditioned survival/event prediction;
- soft regime codebooks and explicit fine/coarse interaction;
- mask-free forward crops and online/EMA role asymmetry;
- orthogonal progression/content coordinates;
- action-decodable latent displacement;
- raw-preserving residual correction;
- whole-entity observability interventions;
- episode-predictive retrieval;
- likelihood-responsibility trajectory mixtures; and
- hard winner-take-all predictor mixtures with router gating, context
  shuffling, codebook control, and realized-transition precision;
- matched-twin effect prediction with supervised and deranged-pair controls;
- raw-bypass task-grounded residual contracts with explicit effect witnesses;
  and
- JEPA-assisted empirical error certificates with raw-only, deranged, and
  direct conformal controls; and
- reconstructive static-teacher asymmetric latent training with an
  equal-capacity deranged-target control.

All candidate inference paths were implemented as bounded CPU/edge paths.
That closes the prior frozen runnable queue and the three locally formulated
one-stack hypotheses. Modality ports and backbone changes are not separate
scientific hypotheses. It does not literally exhaust every published
objective family; the techniques below remain conditional on prerequisites
that the current product lane does not satisfy.

## Recommended next target

The next target is not another generic JEPA paper port.

1. Run the raw rank-32 and direct retrieval baselines in a bounded shadow
   alerting system with explicit abstention and operator feedback.
2. Keep data collection inside the declared stack. Prefer new randomized
   matched interventions, worker-topology variation, and explicit operational
   contract violations that test the alerting lane directly.
3. Measure real missing-channel frequency, recovery trajectories, and
   residual heteroscedasticity. Open T-JEPA, BiJEPA, or probabilistic belief
   work only if those measurements satisfy their prerequisites.
4. Revisit HEPA only after event diversity and treatment coverage materially
   change; revisit MoP only with repeated conditionally identical contexts or
   an environment that can verify several valid successors.

Do not start another JEPA trainer on the current evidence. The locally
formulated PairEffect, task-grounded Contract, and Error-Certificate tracers
have now all failed their matched controls. Reopen a specific conditional
family only after its measured prerequisite appears—for example real
missing-channel incidence for T-JEPA, adequately represented recovery for
BiJEPA, or heteroscedastic/multimodal residuals for belief models.

## Evidence boundary

All results are open-development evidence from the existing corpus. They
support recipe rejection and prioritization; they do not support production
paging, autonomous remediation, broad claims about JEPA research, or claims
about other stochastic domains.
