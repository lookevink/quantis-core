# Evaluate the unimplemented JEPA families

## Destination

Execute the materially distinct unimplemented JEPA families in descending
probability of success under one shared evidence contract. Finish with either
a promotion candidate for fresh sealed confirmation or a bounded negative
disposition for every family.

## Notes

- This map explicitly includes execution, not only planning.
- Use the repository's evidence-first preregistration and artifact conventions.
- Retain every completed tracer runner and assessor with its result record.
  Non-production experiment code is not disposable reproduction code.
- Keep published artifact directories immutable; reruns always use a fresh
  output directory.
- Use TDD at agreed public seams and review each implementation before commit.
- A lower JEPA loss is not success. A candidate must improve observable
  prediction, alert-policy utility, or investigation utility over its declared
  non-JEPA baselines.
- Start with a soft regime-codebook JEPA because it most directly addresses the
  repeated effective-rank and state-retention failures.
- Reuse open development data for tracer experiments. Do not collect fresh
  sealed evidence until a fixed recipe passes tracer and robustness gates.

## Decisions so far

- [Freeze the shared JEPA evaluation contract](001-freeze-shared-evaluation-contract.md) — All tracers share six public seams, topology-transfer evidence, matched controls, pure assessment, and lane-specific value gates.
- [Test a soft regime-codebook JEPA tracer](002-regime-codebook-jepa-tracer.md) — Broad code usage and improved state probing did not preserve effect prediction or alert calibration; reject the recipe and test event-native information next.
- [Test an event-native trace JEPA tracer](003-event-native-trace-jepa-tracer.md) — A compact non-collapsed trace encoder learned the bounded path grammar, but added no aligned alert or investigation value over its shuffled null.
- [Freeze the multi-hypothesis JEPA scoring contract](004-freeze-multi-hypothesis-scoring-contract.md) — Proper mixture scoring, calibration, anti-duplication, matched controls, abstention, and topology-transfer gates now separate useful forecast modes from added capacity.
- [Test a multi-hypothesis trajectory JEPA tracer](005-test-multi-hypothesis-jepa-tracer.md) — Distinct alternatives emerged, but the JEPA mixture lost to its supervised control and badly regressed raw prediction; reject before calibration or value-lane work.
- [Test an exact SIGReg LeJEPA tracer](006-test-exact-sigreg-lejepa-tracer.md) — Exact official-minimal SIGReg broadened latent rank but worsened observable-state probing, earned zero correction gain, and detected no treatment trajectories; reject the substitution while retaining its runner and artifact.
- [Freeze the complete multi-view LeJEPA telemetry contract](007-freeze-complete-lejepa-telemetry-contract.md) — Test LeJEPA as a frozen representation candidate with pair-blocked semantic views, exact convex loss, matched ablations, and downstream action-probe gates rather than accepting loss or rank alone.
- [Test the complete multi-view LeJEPA telemetry tracer](008-test-complete-multi-view-lejepa-tracer.md) — The genuine predictor-free objective learned a restorable and state-accessible representation, but lost to masked reconstruction, nearly doubled raw downstream-effect error, and won only half of held-out pairs; reject before robustness or a two-stage action model.
- [Freeze and test retrieval-JEPA evidence utility](009-freeze-retrieval-jepa-utility-contract.md) — The episode-predictive query map was causal, restorable, and edge-feasible, but raw telemetry and PCA retrieved every transfer mechanism while the JEPA reached only 40% hit@1 and abstained from every treatment; reject the recipe and retain direct retrieval as the stronger investigation baseline.
- [Determine the minimum cross-run and cross-stack corpus diversity](010-determine-cross-stack-corpus-diversity.md) — Seven corpus records collapse to one logical checkout-stack family. A role-clean tracer needs six stacks and at least 765 additional matched pairs; do not fit a cross-stack JEPA until that acquisition prerequisite is met.
- [Audit the remaining JEPA frontier and select the next tracer](011-audit-remaining-jepa-frontier.md) — The remaining runnable mechanisms are HEPA, the complete SC-JEPA interaction, CF-JEPA, SD-JEPA, Delta-JEPA, exact LeWorldModel plus a bounded geometry screen, Causal-JEPA, and MoP-JEPA. HEPA is first because it is alert-native; modality/backbone ports are not separate hypotheses.
- [Test a horizon-conditioned event-predictive JEPA alert tracer](012-test-hepa-event-alert-tracer.md) — HEPA learned a monotone, state-rich, edge-feasible probability surface and improved Brier over its deranged null, but detected only 50% of treatments, tied the null, and trailed simpler controls; reject this exact recipe.
- [Test the complete SC-JEPA codebook × multi-resolution interaction](013-test-complete-sc-jepa-interaction.md) — The complete cell had negligible Brier interaction, zero detection interaction, no treatment detections, collapsed per-entity code use, and 2.73× matched-PCA state error; reject the recipe and proceed to CF-JEPA.
- [Test CF-JEPA mask-free multi-horizon alerting](014-test-cf-jepa-alert-tracer.md) — The EMA target became smoother, lower rank, and state-rich, but three zones lost narrowly to one zone, all neural routes tied at 90% detection, and the 10% control false-alarm rate exceeded the frozen ceiling; reject this recipe and proceed to SD-JEPA.
- [Test SD-JEPA progression/content telemetry](015-test-sd-jepa-alert-tracer.md) — The split made angular change more event-sensitive than A0, but the designated coordinates did not encode progression and the calibrated transfer policy emitted no alerts; reject the recipe and proceed to Delta-JEPA.
- [Test Delta-JEPA action-sensitive displacement](016-test-delta-jepa-action-displacement.md) — The displacement was state-rich and predicted observable change, but endpoint concatenation decoded treatment actions much better and every neural cell nearly doubled raw transfer effect error; reject the recipe and proceed to the exact LeWorldModel geometry screen.
- [Test the LeWorldModel bounded geometry screen](017-test-leworld-geometry-screen.md) — No regularized cell was selection-safe. SPHERE-JEPA won the diagnostic selection by a negligible margin, then lost transfer value and state information; ambient and Sub-JEPA collapsed without meeting the UR-JEPA prerequisite. Reject and proceed to Causal-JEPA.
- [Test Causal-JEPA's entity intervention](018-test-causal-jepa-entity-intervention.md) — Whole-entity masking learned completion beyond persistence but lost to matched coordinate-time masking, regressed raw held-topology effects by more than threefold, and won half of transfer pairs. Reject and proceed to MoP-JEPA.
- [Test MoP-JEPA hard-assigned predictors](019-test-mop-jepa-hard-assignment.md) — In progress under a frozen hard-WTA, context-dependence, transition-precision, proper-score, and edge-safety contract.

## Not yet specified

- T-JEPA missing-channel robustness, V-JEPA 2.1 deep self-supervision,
  BiJEPA, Koopman-constrained prediction, Phys-JEPA, and seq-JEPA remain
  conditional ablations with the prerequisites recorded in the
  [frontier audit](../../research/jepa-frontier-technique-audit-2026.md).
- Specify the complete two-stage V-JEPA-style action/planning pipeline only
  after an encoder earns frozen-state promotion.
- Fit variational or generative latent futures only after a selection-role
  diagnostic demonstrates irreducible conditional uncertainty or a
  predictably nonzero deterministic residual.
- CHARM and other cross-stack semantic methods remain blocked by ticket 010's
  acquisition prerequisite.

## Next target

- Freeze and test MoP-JEPA's hard best-of-K predictor specialization against
  the prior likelihood-trained mixture, supervised best-of-K, single-head,
  and raw controls. Require proper mixture and observable value, not merely
  head diversity.

## Out of scope

- Production paging or autonomous remediation before sealed confirmation and
  a sustained shadow evaluation.
- Re-running superseded invalid artifacts as scientific evidence.
- Large architecture sweeps that alter several hypotheses at once.
