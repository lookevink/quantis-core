## Resolution comment

Resolved on 2026-07-28 after the user approved exchangeable complete-trajectory
hypotheses, proper mixture log scoring with energy-score protection, and
continuation under the recommended defaults.

The complete decision is recorded in
[`Multi-hypothesis JEPA scoring contract v1`](../../specs/multi-hypothesis-jepa-scoring-contract-v1.md).
It adds a four-component mixture distribution with an exact moment-matched
compatibility view; raw selection followed by two-scalar probability
calibration; permutation-invariant support and moment-collapse checks; and
single-component, capacity-matched, and supervised-mixture controls.

Forecast ambiguity cannot page by itself. Hidden-action alerting scores the
observation under the complete no-action mixture, while uncertain action
attribution abstains. A family-specific probabilistic lane requires
held-out-topology log-score improvement, energy-score non-inferiority,
calibration coverage, useful component separation, and point-prediction
safety before robustness work.
